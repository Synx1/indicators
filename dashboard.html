How It Trades

Engine. A z-score model prices how far spot sits from the strike given realized volatility and time left, and picks a side only at ≥85% confidence. Then 2 of 4 indicators (RSI, EMA9/EMA20 cross, Bollinger mid, VWAP) must agree before it acts.

Entry. Only fills priced 25¢–80¢. 30 contracts flat. Max 3 positions at once.

Exit. Cash out at 97¢ if the market gets there; otherwise hold to settlement. No stop-loss.

Universe.

🛡️ Correlation Guard

15-min crypto moves as one asset class, so N same-direction bets settling in the same window are really one leveraged bet. The bot allows one position per direction per settlement window — an opposite-direction bet in the same window is still fine (it hedges).

This is the fix for the day three same-window DOWN bets lost together ($100→$35).

🎯 The 0.80 Cap

A binary bought at price p needs ~p win-rate just to break even. The 85–90¢ band is net negative even at ~90% win (you risk 85¢ to make 15¢). Full replay: the 75–80¢ bucket is the money maker (+$458, 90% win), so entries cap at 80¢.

🔬 What We Tested & Rejected

Stop-loss — every level loses vs. holding to settlement (baseline +$718 beats best stop +$420). Losers often recover; stopping locks them in.

Early take-profit — cashing at 90¢ instead of 97¢ costs ~$305 per 343 trades. Only rescues a handful of losers while trimming every winner.

Confidence-scaled sizing — engine is decently calibrated but $/trade isn't monotonic in confidence, so scaling would misallocate. Size stays flat at 30.

Backtested over 343 entries / ~2,000 markets. Two big levers already shipped: the entry cap and the correlation guard.
